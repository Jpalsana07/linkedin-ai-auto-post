We rebuilt the checkout flow.
The problem was simple: one service needed to change when pricing changed, without breaking the rest of the app.

We tested REST and GraphQL for it.

REST gave us 7 endpoints, and every new screen meant another round of versioning, docs, and client bugs.
GraphQL let the frontend ask for exactly the fields it needed, which cut one mobile payload from 84 KB to 19 KB.

But the real difference showed up in week 2.

With REST, a small pricing change took 3 days because three teams had to coordinate.
With GraphQL, the same change shipped in one afternoon because the contract was one schema — the shared menu of data.

I used to think GraphQL was the fancier choice.
It wasn’t.

For this problem, GraphQL won because it made the system easier to change, not just easier to query.

That is the lesson: choose the tool that reduces coordination, not the one that looks cleaner in a demo.

What tool do you think wins more often in real teams: REST or GraphQL?
