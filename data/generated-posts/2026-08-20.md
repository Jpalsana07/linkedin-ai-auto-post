# LinkedIn Post

Topic: LLM Cost Optimization

Date: 2026-08-20

---

$18,000 in tokens

We were building an AI support bot for a product with thousands of daily tickets.

The question was simple:
do we send the whole conversation every time, or trim it down?

In plain English, this is about how much memory the bot gets before it answers.

More memory usually means better answers.
More memory also means more tokens, and tokens are the bill.

The tradeoff was clear.

Send everything, and the bot had the full story.
But a long chat plus docs could push one request past 20,000 tokens.

Trim aggressively, and costs dropped fast.
But we started cutting out the one detail the customer actually cared about.

We chose a middle path:
keep the last few turns, plus a tiny summary generated by GPT-4o mini.

That cut our monthly spend by 62%.

The reason was not just money.
Our real constraint was latency.
Support agents would wait, and every extra second made the product feel broken.

So the lesson stuck:
optimize for the bottleneck, not the bill.

Sometimes the cheapest prompt is the one that feels a little too small.
That can be fine for internal tools, and wrong for anything where perfect recall matters.

Where would you draw the line between cheaper prompts and better memory?
