Postgres was our bottleneck.

We’re building a support dashboard for small teams.
The people using it are non-technical ops managers who just want fast search and fewer tabs.

This week I shipped the PostgreSQL layer for the first real users.

I added partial indexes for the 2 queries that mattered most.
That cut one page load from 840ms to 110ms.

I also moved the audit log into a separate table instead of stuffing everything into one giant row.
That made writes predictable again.

But one thing is still broken:
our sync job keeps timing out when a customer has more than 80,000 events.

The painful lesson this week was simple.

Postgres usually isn’t slow.
My schema was.

I kept blaming the database because that felt technical.
The fix was to make the data shape match the product shape.

What’s the cleanest Postgres mistake you only spotted after users hit it?
