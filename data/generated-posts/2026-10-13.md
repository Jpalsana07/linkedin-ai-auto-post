# LinkedIn Post

Topic: LLM Cost Optimization

Date: 2026-10-13

---

Our LLM bill doubled.

We were building a customer support assistant.
The product was fine.
The invoice was not.

The choice was simple on paper:
send every user message to the biggest model, or route only the hard ones there.

That sounds tiny.
It isn’t.

The expensive model was better at messy edge cases.
But most questions were boring:
password resets, billing dates, shipping status.
Paying top dollar for those was like hiring a surgeon to open jars.

We tried caching, but it only helped repeated prompts.
We tried shorter prompts, but the real cost was still the model call.
So we switched to a cheaper model first, and only escalated to GPT-4o when the answer was unclear.

That cut spend by 58% in 2 weeks.

The tradeoff:
cheaper routing adds one more decision before the answer.
If your product lives on speed and simplicity, that extra hop can hurt.
If your product handles lots of repetitive, low-risk questions, it’s worth it.

The constraint most people miss:
cost is not the only bill.
Every extra model call also adds latency, and users feel that before finance does.

The lesson:
don’t optimize LLM cost by chasing the cheapest token.
Optimize by asking which questions deserve the expensive brain.

Where would you spend the extra latency: on first-pass speed or better answers?
