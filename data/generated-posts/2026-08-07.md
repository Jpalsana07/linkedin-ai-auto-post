# LinkedIn Post

Topic: LLM Cost Optimization

Date: 2026-08-07

---

Built for support teams.

I’m shipping an AI assistant that answers internal product questions before they ping an engineer.

This week I cut our LLM spend by 41%.

The change was simple on paper:
we stopped sending every user message to GPT-4o and added a cheap route first.

If the question was about pricing, docs, or a known bug, a smaller model handled it.
Only messy edge cases went to the bigger model.

I used OpenAI’s API and a tiny rules layer.
The rules layer — a set of if/then checks — took 80 lines of code.

What broke:
some long, emotional customer messages got misrouted.
The cheap model saw “refund” and missed the real issue.
That created 17 bad handoffs in one day.

So the lesson this week was clear:
cost optimization only works when you protect the hard cases, not when you squeeze everything through the cheapest path.

I’d rather spend $0.03 on the right answer than save $0.02 and make support slower.
