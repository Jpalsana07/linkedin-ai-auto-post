3 retries changed everything.

I’m building an AI support agent for a small SaaS team.
It helps founders and support reps answer repeat customer questions faster.

This week I shipped a retry flow with a tool schema, the menu of actions the agent can take.
Before that, one bad API rate limit hit could stall a whole conversation.

We tested it on 1,200 real support threads.
First-response accuracy went from 71% to 84%.
Average handle time dropped from 6 minutes to 3.8.

The ugly part: the agent still fails on messy tickets.
If a customer mixes billing, login, and “please cancel” in one message, it often picks the wrong next step.
That shows up most with Stripe billing cases and password resets.

I used to think the win was making the agent smarter.
This week proved something simpler.

The win is making the agent recover cleanly when it gets confused.

That’s the lesson I keep seeing: in AI agents, recovery matters more than brilliance.

What kind of failure would you rather ship with: a slow answer, or a confident wrong one?
