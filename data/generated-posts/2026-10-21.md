# LinkedIn Post

Topic: SaaS Build-in-Public Metrics

Date: 2026-10-21

---

I killed our metric dashboard.

We were building a SaaS for founders.
The promise was simple: show your build-in-public numbers without a spreadsheet.
Then the dashboard made us feel busier, not clearer.

For 3 weeks, I watched daily signups, activation rate, and churn in Mixpanel, a product analytics tool.
The charts looked polished.
The story was wrong.

I kept adding more graphs.
Revenue, trial starts, email opens, referral clicks.
Every new chart gave me one more thing to explain and one less thing to act on.

The root cause was embarrassingly simple:
I was tracking what was easy to measure, not what would change the product.

Once I cut the dashboard down to 4 numbers, the noise dropped hard.
We found the real issue in one afternoon: users were signing up, but not reaching the first useful action.

That was the postmortem.
Build-in-public metrics are only useful when they help you make one decision.

If a number cannot change what you do next, it is just decoration.

What metric have you seen people obsess over while missing the real problem?
