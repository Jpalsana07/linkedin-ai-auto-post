# LinkedIn Post

Topic: LLM Cost Optimization

Date: 2026-09-03

---

Stop chasing cheaper tokens.

The common advice is simple:
pick a smaller model, trim the prompt, and call it optimization.

We built a support assistant for a product team.
The bill hit $1,840 in one month.

Everyone wanted the same fix:
swap GPT-4o for a cheaper model and hope quality held.

We tried it.
Answers got shorter, but so did trust.

The real waste was not the model.
It was sending the same 9-page policy doc into every request, even when the user asked about one billing rule.

We changed the system so it only pulled 2 relevant paragraphs first.
Then we cached the boring repeat answers.
Then we stopped asking the model to draft things a rules engine could decide in 3 lines.

Cost dropped 62% in 11 days.
Quality went up, because the model got less junk and more signal.

The strongest counterargument is fair:
sometimes the model really is the expensive part.

But most teams I’ve seen are paying for repetition, not intelligence.

The lesson: reduce the work around the model before you downgrade the model itself.

What have you seen save more money: a smaller model, or less context?
