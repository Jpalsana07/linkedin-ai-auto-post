This week’s SaaS update.

I’m building a tiny invoicing app for freelance designers.

This week I shipped Stripe checkout, CSV export, and a cleaner onboarding flow.

We sent the first public version to 37 people.

Signup-to-first-invoice moved from 18% to 31%.

That sounds good until you look at the broken part.

11 people started onboarding and quit at the same screen:
the “connect your account” step.

That step is the tax form of the product.
Nobody hates the app.
They hate the moment where work feels like work.

I also learned something from the numbers:
people who exported a CSV were 3x more likely to come back the next day.

So the lesson for me this week is simple:
don’t celebrate signups before you fix the first annoying step.

Growth usually hides in the place where people almost gave up.

What’s the first screen in your product that quietly loses people?
