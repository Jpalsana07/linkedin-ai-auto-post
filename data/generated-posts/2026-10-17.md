# LinkedIn Post

Topic: Startup Lessons

Date: 2026-10-17

---

We lost the launch week.

We were building our first paid product.
The goal was simple: get 20 customers before the demo day investors were watching.

Instead, I merged a “small” change on Friday night.
It looked harmless.
By Monday, checkout was failing for half the users.

The worst part wasn’t the bug itself.
It was that we’d spent 6 weeks polishing features people hadn’t asked for, while the one thing that mattered was fragile.

We fixed the code in a few hours.
But the damage was already done: support emails, a delayed launch, and a very quiet Slack channel.

That week taught me one thing I still use today:

Shipping faster matters less than shipping the thing people actually depend on.

Not the flashiest feature.
Not the nicest dashboard.
The part that lets someone pay, sign in, or get value without friction.

What’s a product mistake you’ve seen that looked small on Friday and expensive on Monday?
