# LinkedIn Post

Topic: MCP and Context Engineering

Date: 2026-10-06

---

MCP shipped on Friday.

I’m building an AI assistant for support teams.
This week I wired it to our internal docs, tickets, and Slack so it can answer with real company context.

The new piece was MCP, the Model Context Protocol — a standard way for the model to ask for tools and data.

Before that, the bot guessed a lot.
It would answer fast, but it would miss policy details or quote stale docs.

After the ship, one metric moved hard:
correct answers on our 50-question support set went from 68% to 84%.

But one thing broke.

When the prompt got too long, the bot started ignoring the newest ticket and clinging to old context.
That means context engineering — deciding what the model sees first — mattered more than adding another tool.

I spent most of the week trimming 12 KB of noisy history down to the 3 lines that actually changed the answer.

That was the real lesson.

With MCP, the hard part is not connecting tools.
It’s choosing the right context, in the right order, every time.
