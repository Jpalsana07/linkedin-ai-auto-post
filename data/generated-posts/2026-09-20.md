# LinkedIn Post

Topic: Career Inflection Moments

Date: 2026-09-20

---

I missed the promotion.

I was building a hiring tool for founders.
It was for teams trying to fill one hard role fast.
And I was sure the product would sell itself.

It didn’t.

We shipped 41 outreach emails, 12 demos, and 0 paid pilots.
The feature set looked strong on paper.
The problem was the pitch.

I kept talking about automation.
Founders heard risk.
They didn’t want a system that “managed candidates.”
They wanted fewer bad resumes and faster decisions.

The root cause was simple:
I was selling my internal model of the product, not the outcome in their head.

So I rewrote the homepage in one afternoon.
No jargon.
No “workflow.”
Just “shortlist better candidates in less time.”

Replies came in that week.

The lesson: when a career move or a product launch fails, the failure is often not the thing itself.
It’s the story you told about it.

What story have you seen kill an otherwise good idea?
