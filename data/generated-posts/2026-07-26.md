Topic: AI Agents

Date: 2026-07-26

48 hours changed everything.

I’m building an AI agent for customer support teams.
This week, it started handling real tickets from a small pilot customer.

We shipped a tighter tool schema — the menu of actions the bot can take — so it could stop guessing and start doing.
It can now look up order status, refund a charge, and hand off to a human with the right context.

The big win: first-response time dropped from 11 minutes to 42 seconds.
That mattered more than any demo.

What broke?
The agent still gets trapped on messy edge cases.
If a customer mixes two problems in one message, it sometimes picks the wrong action and needs a reset.
We saw that in 17% of test conversations this week.

The lesson is simple:
agents get useful when they can do fewer things very reliably.

That’s the part people skip.
They want the bot to feel smart.
Customers just want the problem solved fast.

What would you trust an agent to do first in support?
