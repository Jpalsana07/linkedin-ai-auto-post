Topic: Agent Reliability

Date: 2026-09-12

---

Our support agent is live.
It helps small teams answer customer emails without hiring a full-time rep.

This week I shipped a new reliability layer for it.

The agent now retries failed tool calls, checks its own output before sending, and stops after 3 bad attempts instead of spinning forever.

That matters because one bad reply can land in a customer inbox.

Before this, the agent answered 68% of tickets correctly in our test set.
After the changes, it hit 81%.

The ugly part: the last 19% still fail in the same places.
It gets confused by refund edge cases and mixed intent emails, like “cancel my plan but keep my data.”

That’s the part I’m working on now.

The lesson is simple:
agent reliability is not about making the model smarter first.
It’s about making failure smaller, faster, and easier to spot.
