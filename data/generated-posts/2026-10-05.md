# LinkedIn Post

Topic: Freelancing with Numbers

Date: 2026-10-05

---

Two tools cost me clients.

I was trying to send invoices faster.

The choice was between Bonsai, a freelance admin app, and Notion plus Stripe, a homemade stack.

Bonsai handled contracts, proposals, invoices, and reminders in one place.

Notion held the docs.
Stripe took payments.
I glued the rest together by hand.

At first, the handmade setup felt cheaper.

It wasn’t.

I spent 6 hours building templates, fixing formatting, and chasing edge cases.
Bonsai took 20 minutes to set up.
It was $24 a month.
The DIY stack looked free, then ate a Saturday every time I onboarded someone new.

The real difference was not features.

It was trust.

Clients understood Bonsai immediately.
They knew where to sign, where to pay, and when to expect the invoice.

My custom flow looked clever.
Clever is expensive when a client just wants to pay you.

The lesson: if the tool touches money and trust, buy the boring one.

What’s a tool you stopped customizing because the client experience mattered more?
