# LinkedIn Post

Topic: Node.js at Scale

Date: 2026-09-05

---

Redis was hiding our bottleneck.

We were running a Node.js API that had to serve 8,000 requests a minute without turning every page load into a wait.

The choice was Redis pub/sub or BullMQ, a job queue for background work.

Redis pub/sub is fast and simple.
It pushes messages right now, but if a worker is down, the message is gone.

BullMQ adds retries, delays, and persistence.
That means a failed email or report still gets processed later, not lost in traffic.

We tested both on one service.

Pub/sub was cheaper to wire up.
We shipped it in 2 days.

But once traffic grew, the missing messages hurt more than the setup speed helped.

BullMQ took an extra week to fit in cleanly.
After that, failed jobs dropped by 93%, and support stopped seeing “I clicked send and nothing happened” complaints.

The lesson: at scale, the best tool is the one that remembers what happened when your system is busy or broken.

I’ll take the slower setup if it buys me fewer lost tasks.

When have you chosen the less flashy tool because it protected the work?
