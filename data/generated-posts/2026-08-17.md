# LinkedIn Post

Topic: Startup Lessons

Date: 2026-08-17

---

I deleted the wrong feature.

We were trying to land our first paid pilot with a customer who cared about speed, not polish.
The demo was in 3 days, and I spent 2 weeks building a dashboard nobody had asked for.

It looked impressive.
It also confused everyone in the room.

The real ask was simpler: upload a file, get an answer, move on.
Instead, I had wrapped it in filters, tabs, and a settings page because I wanted the product to feel complete.

On demo day, the customer kept clicking the one thing we had buried.
We lost the pilot.

That night I cut the product down to the smallest useful flow.
One upload.
One result.
No extra choices.

The next demo got a yes.

The lesson I keep paying for: if people can't find the first win fast, your "better" product is just more work.

What feature have you seen hide the real value?
