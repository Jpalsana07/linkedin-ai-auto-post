# LinkedIn Post

Topic: SaaS Build-in-Public Metrics

Date: 2026-09-30

---

Built for small teams.

I’m building a SaaS that helps solo founders and tiny teams turn messy customer calls into clear next steps.

This week I shipped the first onboarding flow.
People can now sign up, paste a call transcript, and get a cleaned-up action list in under 2 minutes.

The metric that moved: activation went from 18% to 31%.
That means more people made it past the first screen and actually used the product.

The broken part is obvious.
35% of users still drop off when they hit the transcript upload.
Most of them aren’t stuck on the product.
They’re stuck on the file itself.

Some recordings come in as .mp3.
Some are Zoom exports.
Some are just a link to a Google Drive folder with three unrelated files inside.

I thought the hard part was the AI.
It wasn’t.

The hard part was making the first 60 seconds feel stupidly simple.

That’s the lesson I keep relearning while building in public:
early growth is usually blocked by confusion, not demand.

What would you fix first: the first click or the final output?
