Topic: LLM Cost Optimization

Date: 2026-07-25

---

GPT-4o was too expensive.

We had a customer support feature that answered 10,000 questions a day.
The problem wasn’t quality.
It was the bill.

So I tested two ways to cut LLM costs: OpenAI GPT-4o mini and Anthropic Claude Haiku 3.
Both are smaller models built for cheaper, faster responses.
Both can handle basic Q&A.
Only one made sense for us.

I wired them into the same prompt, same retrieval setup, same test set of 500 real support tickets.
GPT-4o mini averaged 280 ms and cost us about $18 per million tokens.
Claude Haiku 3 was faster at 190 ms and came in closer to $8 per million tokens.

Haiku won.

Why?
Because our use case was simple: short answers, high volume, low drama.
The speed was nice.
The lower token cost was the real win.

The lesson: when the job is repetitive, pick the cheaper model that clears the bar.
Don’t pay premium prices for words your users skim.
