# LinkedIn Post

Topic: Freelancing with Numbers

Date: 2026-08-26

---

I almost overbuilt my freelance stack.

I was setting up how clients would pay, not writing code.
That sounds boring, but it decides when money hits your account.

The choice was simple: Stripe subscriptions or one-off invoices.

Subscriptions look cleaner.
They promise predictable cash, fewer reminders, and less admin.

Invoices looked messier.
But they matched how I actually worked: project by project, with scope that changed every week.

I chose invoices.

Why?
Because I was freelancing while still holding a 20-hour side contract.
I needed cash to land fast, not “eventually.”
A subscription that starts next month is nice.
A $4,000 invoice paid in 5 days keeps the lights on.

The tradeoff is real.
Subscriptions are better when the same work repeats every month.
Invoices are better when every client brings a different problem and a different timeline.

The lesson: pick the payment model that matches your uncertainty, not your ego.

If your work is changing every week, monthly billing can become a trap.

Where have you seen recurring billing help, and where has it made life harder?
