Topic: MCP and Context Engineering

Date: 2026-09-09

MCP is not the bottleneck.

The hot part is context.

A lot of people are treating Model Context Protocol like a magic plug.
Connect the model to every tool, and the agent will somehow get smart.

We tried that with a support assistant.
It had access to Slack, Jira, and our docs.
It still answered slowly and confidently in the wrong direction.

The problem was not the connector.
It was the pile of junk we kept handing the model.

After 3 days of trimming prompts, adding a 12-line context builder, and dropping two noisy tools, answer quality jumped.
Fewer tool calls.
Less confusion.
Faster replies.

That’s context engineering: deciding what the model sees, in what order, and why.
Not “give it everything.”
Give it the right few things.

The strongest counterargument is real:
MCP makes integrations easier, and that matters a lot.
If a tool is hard to connect, nobody uses it.

But once the pipes exist, context is what decides whether the model is useful or just well-connected.

The lesson: better inputs beat more integrations.
