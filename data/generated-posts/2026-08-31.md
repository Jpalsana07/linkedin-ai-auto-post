# LinkedIn Post

Topic: LLM in Production

Date: 2026-08-31

---

We shipped the chatbot to 120 users.
It answers support questions for a small SaaS team.

This week I pushed three things live.

First, I moved the LLM from ad hoc prompts to a fixed OpenAI response format, which is just a stricter way to force cleaner outputs.

Second, I added LangSmith, a trace viewer that shows every step the model took.

Third, I cut the average reply time from 4.8 seconds to 2.1 seconds.

That last number mattered more than I expected.

Users did not care that the prompt was prettier.
They cared that the answer arrived before they alt-tabbed away.

What is still broken?

The model is still too eager to sound certain when it should ask for help.
In 7% of cases, it invents a policy detail instead of saying “I’m not sure.”

So the lesson for me this week was simple:
shipping LLMs in production is mostly about removing chances to be wrong, not making the model sound smarter.

What do you think matters more in production: faster answers or fewer confident mistakes?
