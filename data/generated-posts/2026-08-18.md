Two tools, one production mess.

We needed a chatbot that could answer product questions without hallucinating.
The choice was between LangChain and LlamaIndex, and the wrong pick meant weeks of lost time.

LangChain gave us a lot of control.
It was great for wiring steps together, but our team spent 9 days debugging prompt chains, retrievers, and memory issues before we had a stable demo.

LlamaIndex was different.
It was built around documents first, which meant our PDF knowledge base, FAQ pages, and support articles got into the app in 2 days instead of 9.

The surprise was not speed alone.
It was that LlamaIndex made the system easier to explain to non-engineers.
“We ask the docs, then answer” is a sentence a PM can repeat.
“Our chain has three agents and a custom retriever” is not.

We shipped with LlamaIndex.

The lesson: when two tools solve the same problem, pick the one that makes the system simpler to understand, not just the one that looks more flexible.
